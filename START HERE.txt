OFFLINE LIST BUILDER – Warhammer 40,000 (11th edition)
=======================================================

1. UNZIP FIRST
   Right-click the .zip -> "Extract All…", and put the folder anywhere you like
   (Desktop, Documents, a USB stick…). Don't run it from inside the zip.

2. START
   Open the extracted folder and double-click  index.html
   It opens in your web browser (Chrome or Edge recommended, Firefox also works).
   No internet connection or installation needed.

3. BUILD A LIST
   Pick a faction, battle size and name -> "Create roster", then "+ Add unit".
   Your lists are saved automatically IN YOUR BROWSER (not in this folder).
   Use "Save file" to back a list up, or to give it to someone else
   (they use "Import list…" on the home screen).

4. PLAY A GAME
   Open a list and press "▶ Play" for a read-only view with wound, CP/VP,
   round/phase and Battle-shock tracking.

5. SHARING LISTS WITH OTHER APPS
   "Export list" gives GW app or WTC tournament text.
   "Import list…" accepts pasted GW app / New Recruit text and
   BattleScribe / New Recruit .rosz files.

6. EDITING THE DATA (optional)
   Open  editor.html  in Chrome or Edge, click "Choose folder…" and pick THIS
   folder (the one containing index.html and wh40k-11e-main). Changes are saved
   into the files; reload the list builder afterwards.

FULL ILLUSTRATED GUIDE: open  help.html  (or press the ? button in the program).
More detail is also in README.txt.
Unit data comes from the community BSData project (github.com/BSData/wh40k-11e);
it is not official and not endorsed by Games Workshop.
